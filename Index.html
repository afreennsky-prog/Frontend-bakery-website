Our Story

Golden Crumb started in a small home kitchen with one family recipe and a big love for the smell of warm bread. Today we bake in small batches, using fresh butter, local ingredients and no shortcuts.

Whether it is a birthday cake, a weekend sourdough or a simple tea-time cookie, every bake is made by hand.
